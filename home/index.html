What Publick is

Publick is a network of free, independent websites. Each one publishes a town's public record every day: meeting agendas and minutes as readable text with summaries, who represents you, 311 requests, what's happening on each street, and budget, schools and housing data.

Independent. Not run by or affiliated with any city or town government.

Is your town next?

Tell us where you'd like Publick to go next. Residents, libraries, local newsrooms, sponsors and city or town staff are all welcome to write.
