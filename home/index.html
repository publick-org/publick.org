What Publick is

Publick is a network of free, independent websites. Each one publishes a town's public record every day: meeting agendas and minutes as readable text with plain-English summaries, 311 requests, what's happening on each street, and budget, schools and housing data.

Independent. Not run by or affiliated with any city or town government.
